IAM Blast Radius

Paste an AWS IAM policy. Everything is analyzed in your browser - nothing is uploaded.

This tool shows the potential blast radius based on the supplied policy context. It does not compute a principal's effective permissions: AWS evaluates identity, resource, and trust policies, permission boundaries, SCPs, and session policies together, and a single policy cannot show that. Findings are graded by certainty.
